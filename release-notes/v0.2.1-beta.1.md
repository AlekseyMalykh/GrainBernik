# GrainBernik v0.2.1-beta.1 — ROCKNIX iPhone audio playback stability

## Fixed / Исправлено

- **Play freeze when iPhone audio went idle.** On RG40XX H / ROCKNIX, playback could stop (both sound and progress bar) about 6–8 seconds after an iPhone video was paused or Final Cut stopped using USB audio. The root cause was a PipeWire graph that could use the higher-priority UAC2 gadget capture node as its clock-driving node. Local PCM playback then stalled and GrainBrick blocked writing to `aplay`.
- **Зависание Play при бездействии iPhone.** При подключённом iPhone звук и полоса воспроизведения Anbernic могли остановиться через 6–8 секунд после прекращения аудио на iPhone. Приоритет драйвера внутреннего динамика H616 повышен до `3000`, чтобы локальное воспроизведение не зависело от USB-входа.
- **Reinstall persistence / Сохранение исправления при переустановке.** The ROCKNIX package builder now bundles the WirePlumber rule and the installer automatically installs it and restarts WirePlumber.

## Verified / Проверено на устройстве

On RG40XX H with ROCKNIX and an iPhone connected: local Play and progress bar keep moving even when the iPhone is silent and Final Cut is closed; iPhone recording and monitoring also work. These are hardware observations, not a full regression certification.

На RG40XX H проверено: локальный Play работает без активности iPhone, запись с iPhone и мониторинг работают. Полный набор регрессионных тестов на устройстве не заявляется.

## Compatibility and scope / Совместимость

- ROCKNIX H700 with the expected built-in sink name `alsa_output._sys_devices_platform_soc_5096000.codec_sound_card0.HiFi__Speaker__sink`.
- No changes to GrainBrick proprietary binaries, presets, the native player, Zoom H1n routing or KNULLI.
- Official GrainBrick app ZIP is required separately; it is **not** redistributed.
- Do not treat the previous experimental `gb_pipewire.py` v4/v5 tests as verified release fixes; this release's proven change is the WirePlumber priority rule.

## Rollback / Откат

Remove `/storage/.config/wireplumber/wireplumber.conf.d/99-grainbrick-driver.conf` and restart WirePlumber or reboot. Existing user data remains untouched.
